Sorry!

Draw a card, ride the slides and bump rivals back to Start in the race to get all four pawns Home.

Rules

Draw, then move OfficialThe card says how far. Land on a rival to bump it back to Start; never on your own pawn.
A 1 or a 2 leaves Start OfficialA 2 always draws again, even when it can't move.
Slides OfficialLand on another colour's triangle to slide to its end, bumping every pawn on the way.
Special cards Official4 goes back 4. 7 can split between two pawns. 10 goes 10 or back 1. 11 goes 11 or swaps with a rival.
Sorry! OfficialA pawn from Start takes a rival's square and sends it back to Start.
Exact count Home OfficialYour safety zone is yours alone. First to get all four pawns Home wins.
