📺 Board Games for Fire TV

Trouble · Sorry! · Monopoly — TV is the board, phones are the controllers

Install with the Downloader app
1
On the Fire TV: Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → Install unknown apps → turn on for Downloader.
2
Open Downloader and enter this link:
maverick5150.github.io/board-games/tv/BoardGamesTV.apk
3
Choose Install, then Open. It’s also in your apps list as Board Games.
Playing
1
Pick a game on the TV — a QR code and 4-letter room code appear.
2
Everyone scans the QR with their phone and joins.
3
Press Start on the TV. Your phone becomes your controller.
Needs Wi-Fi/internet. The remote’s Back button steps back through the menus.
